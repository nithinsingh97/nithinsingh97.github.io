B2B Lead Generation and Data Research

Targeted, researched B2B lead lists built from public sources and matched to your ideal customer profile.

What is it?

B2B lead generation, as I deliver it, means researching and compiling lists of companies and decision-makers that match your ideal customer profile, using public sources such as company websites, business directories and professional listings, then cleaning and structuring the results for your CRM.

Overview

A lead list is only as good as its targeting. Before collecting anything, I agree on your ideal customer profile with you: industry, location, company size signals, job titles and any must-have criteria. That definition decides which sources to use and which records to keep.

I combine automated extraction with manual research where it matters. Directories and websites provide the base list, and each record is then enriched and checked: is the company still active, does the website work, does the contact match the role you asked for. You get a list you can import straight into your CRM.

Business use cases

  • Outbound sales lists for a specific industry and region
  • Account lists for account-based marketing
  • Partner, distributor or supplier discovery
  • Event, conference and exhibitor lists
  • Refreshing and filling gaps in an existing CRM dataset

Example deliverables

  • Company name, website, industry, location, phone and public contact channels
  • Contact name, job title and LinkedIn or profile URL where publicly available
  • Segmentation columns that match your ICP criteria
  • CRM-ready CSV (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive or custom column order)

Technologies used

  • Python
  • Web scraping
  • Data research
  • Pandas
  • Email format checks
  • Excel

How the project works

  1. Brief: share the source, fields, estimated records, output format and deadline.
  2. Feasibility check: I inspect the site and confirm approach, timeline and price.
  3. Sample: you approve a sample of the output.
  4. Full extraction: I run, clean and validate the data.
  5. Delivery: you receive the final file, plus the script if agreed.

Frequently asked questions

Do you guarantee email deliverability?

I don't promise a fixed deliverability rate. I collect publicly listed emails and can run format and domain checks, and I recommend running a dedicated verification service before any campaign.

Can you target specific job titles?

Yes. Tell me the roles you want, for example operations managers or founders, and I filter and research contacts that match.

Where does the data come from?

Public sources: company websites, business directories, industry associations, public listings and similar. The sources are agreed for each project.
