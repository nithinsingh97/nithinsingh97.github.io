What is it?

Business directory scraping extracts company and professional listings, such as name, category, address, phone, website, email and profile details, from online directories like Yellow Pages, industry associations, chambers of commerce and professional registers.

Overview

Directories are among the most reliable lead sources because they are organised by category and location, and often curated by an association or registry. Getting a complete copy is harder: results are capped per search, split across thousands of pages, and the useful fields often sit on separate profile pages.

I work through the directory systematically, by category, region or postcode, follow every profile link and capture the full record. Results from overlapping searches are merged and deduplicated, and fields such as phone numbers and addresses are standardised.

Business use cases

  • Lead lists by trade or profession and location
  • Professional registers such as lawyers, accountants and consultants
  • Association and chamber of commerce member lists
  • Supplier and manufacturer discovery

Example deliverables

  • Company or professional name, category, full address, phone, website and email where listed
  • Profile URL and any extra profile fields such as services, opening hours and licences
  • Search-segment columns (category, region) for filtering
  • Deduplicated, standardised Excel or CSV

Technologies used

  • Python
  • Requests
  • BeautifulSoup
  • Scrapy
  • Playwright
  • Pandas

Frequently asked questions

Directories limit results per search. How do you get everything?

I split searches into smaller segments, such as postcodes, cities or subcategories, until each stays under the cap, then merge and deduplicate.

Can you scrape directories in other languages and countries?

Yes. I've worked with directories in several countries and languages, and I handle local address and phone formats.

Do you include data from the profile pages?

Yes, if you need it. Visiting each profile is usually where the most valuable fields are.
