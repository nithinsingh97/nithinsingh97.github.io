Extracting Amazon Product Reviews into Excel

Completed client project: 415 product review records organised into a four-column Excel workbook.

Evidence-based case study

This is a completed client project shared with permission. Client identity and individual personal details are withheld.

Client requirement

The completed project involved organising Amazon product review information into a structured spreadsheet for downstream analysis.

Verified deliverable

The supplied Excel workbook contains 415 review records in one worksheet, with these four columns:

  • Review
  • Name
  • Date
  • Rating

The workbook provides direct evidence of the extracted dataset and its structure. Reviewer names and review text are not reproduced publicly to protect privacy and avoid republishing source content.

Project outcome

A structured Excel dataset of review text, reviewer name, review date and rating, with one record per row. The sample does not establish the extraction library, total products covered or collection duration, so those details are not claimed.
