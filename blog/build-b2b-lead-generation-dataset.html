How to Build a B2B Lead Generation Dataset

A step-by-step method for building a B2B lead dataset: define your ICP, choose public sources, extract, enrich, deduplicate, verify and export to your CRM.

NSNithin Singh Published 2026-11-149 min readLead Generation
Quick answer

To build a B2B lead generation dataset, define your ideal customer profile, choose public sources that list those companies, extract a base list, enrich each company with website, contact and segmentation data, deduplicate and verify the records, then export in your CRM's import format.

1. Define your ideal customer profile

Start with the target, not the tool. Write down:

  • Industries and sub-industries
  • Countries, regions or cities
  • Size signals such as employee count, number of locations or presence of a careers page
  • Target job titles and seniority
  • Exclusions such as existing customers, competitors or franchises

Every later decision, including sources, fields and filters, follows from this.

2. Choose the right public sources

SourceGood for
Google MapsLocal and SMB businesses by category and area
Business directories and Yellow PagesTrades and professions by location
Industry associationsVerified members of a specific industry
Event and exhibitor listsCompanies active in a market right now
Company websitesEmails, phones, team pages and enrichment

3. Extract a base list

Collect company name, website, location and category from your primary source. Keep the source URL for every record. See the Google Maps data extraction guide for one of the most common starting points.

4. Enrich

  • Visit each website for publicly listed emails, phone numbers and social links.
  • Find decision-makers from team pages and public professional profiles.
  • Add segmentation columns that match your ICP.

5. Deduplicate and verify

Deduplicate on website domain first. It is the most reliable company key. Then standardise phone formats, fix capitalisation and remove obviously invalid emails. Before sending any campaign, run emails through a dedicated verification service. No lead list should claim a guaranteed deliverability rate. The Pandas cleaning guide covers the techniques.

6. Deliver in CRM format

Match your CRM's import template column for column: HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive or custom. Separate company and contact records if your CRM uses associations.

A note on compliance

B2B outreach is still regulated. GDPR and UK GDPR apply to personal data such as names and work emails, and rules like CAN-SPAM govern commercial email. Use data for relevant outreach, include opt-outs and keep records of your sources. This is general information, not legal advice.

Frequently asked questions

How many leads should a B2B list have?

Enough to match your outreach capacity and test properly. A smaller, accurately targeted list usually outperforms a large generic one.

Should I buy a lead list or build one?

Bought lists are often outdated and shared with competitors. A custom-built list matches your ICP and comes from current public sources.
