FAQ

Questions clients ask first

What does a web scraping specialist do?

A web scraping specialist builds programs that collect information from websites and turn it into structured data such as Excel, CSV or JSON. Nithin Singh writes custom Python scrapers, cleans and validates the results, and delivers data in the exact format a business needs.

How do I start a project with Nithin Singh?

Send the website URL, the fields you need, an estimated record count, your preferred output format and your deadline, either through the project quote form on this site or by messaging on Fiverr. You'll receive a plan and, for most projects, a sample before the full run.

Which countries do you work with?

Clients from anywhere. Work is delivered remotely, and communication is in English. The site is aimed at businesses in the USA, UK, Canada, Australia and Europe, among others.

Do you offer a sample before the full dataset?

Yes. For most projects a small sample is delivered first so you can confirm fields, format and quality.

Which output formats do you deliver?

Excel (.xlsx), CSV, JSON, Google Sheets, and CRM-ready files for tools like HubSpot or Salesforce, with your preferred column order.

Do you scrape any kind of data?

No. Only publicly accessible data, collected responsibly. Requests to bypass logins, paywalls or security measures, or that would breach data protection law, are declined.
